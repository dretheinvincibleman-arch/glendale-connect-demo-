Why a city would pay for this

Glendale Connect keeps reports public and tied to a specific neighborhood, so council offices get a live read on what each district actually cares about, instead of piecing it together from whoever shows up to a meeting.

It also gives residents a reason to come back between issues — discussions and polls turn a one-time report into an ongoing relationship with their council office, not just a closed ticket.

Advertise on Glendale Connect

Reach engaged Glendale residents — the neighbors who show up, vote in polls, and read what's happening in their district. Ads are clearly labeled and never shown on city-licensed instances.

Two ways to monetize this

Direct-sold ads — you contact local businesses yourself and drop their card into the ad slots below. You keep 100% and set your own rate.

Google AdSense — once you publish this site with its own domain, apply for a free AdSense account, then paste your ad unit code into the marked slot in the HTML. Google fills the space automatically and pays you per view/click. Approval usually needs some real traffic and a live domain first.
